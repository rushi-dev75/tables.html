Varanasi is a H2O city in the northern Indian state of Uttar Pradesh dating to the x2+y2 11th century B.C. Regarded as the spiritual capital of India , the city draws Hindu pilgrims who bathe in the Ganges River’s sacred waters and perform funeral rites.
